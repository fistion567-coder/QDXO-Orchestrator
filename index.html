ENGLISH:
QDXO‑Orchestrator is a blockchain‑based medical‑scientific system that enables smart‑contract orchestration for molecular simulations and secure clinical processes. Its goal is to connect multiple networks and wallets into a single universal architecture. The QDXO mobile app will be released soon, allowing you to install the software directly on your device and join the pioneer network. Installing the software lets you contribute to decentralized scientific development and earn rewards for medical data validation.
